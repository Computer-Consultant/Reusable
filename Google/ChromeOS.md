# Proper Long-Term Shutdown of a Chromebook to Protect the Battery

To safely store a Chromebook for months without damaging the battery, you should **prepare it for long-term storage** by charging it to about **80%** and putting it into a **battery disconnect (ship mode)** so it won’t discharge further while unplugged [Google Support](https://support.google.com/chrome/a/answer/9139543?hl=en-EN).

## Step-by-Step: Entering Battery Disconnect Mode

1. **Charge to ~80%**

    - Connect the Chromebook to a charger and let it charge until the battery is around 80% full. This prevents deep discharge and keeps the battery in a healthy state [Google Support](https://support.google.com/chrome/a/answer/9139543?hl=en-EN).
2. **Connect to charger and turn on**

    - Plug in the charger and power on the Chromebook.
3. **Enter battery disconnect state**

    - **Without a G key**: Hold **Refresh** and **Power** at the same time.
    - **With a G key**: Hold **Refresh** and tap **Power**.
    - While holding these keys, **remove the power cable** from the device, then release the keys [Google Support](https://support.google.com/chrome/a/answer/9139543?hl=en-EN).
4. **Confirm it’s off**

    - After about 20 seconds, press the **Power** button.
    - The Chromebook should **not** power on, even though it still has a charge. This confirms it’s in battery disconnect mode [Google Support](https://support.google.com/chrome/a/answer/9139543?hl=en-EN).

## Why This Works

- **Prevents deep discharge**: Even when off, Chromebook batteries slowly lose charge. Storing at 80% avoids reaching 0% and potential permanent battery failure [Google Support](https://support.google.com/chrome/a/answer/9139543?hl=en-EN).
- **Reduces wear**: Constant charging/discharging during storage shortens battery life. Battery disconnect mode stops this cycle [Google Support](https://support.google.com/chrome/a/answer/9139543?hl=en-EN).
- **Stable state**: The device remains in a stable, powered-off state until you plug it back in [Lenovo Support](https://support.lenovo.com/us/en/solutions/ht504333-how-to-prepare-a-chromebook-for-long-term-storage-by-placing-the-machine-in-ship-mode-chromebook).

## Storage Best Practices

- Avoid stacking more than 7 devices on top of each other to prevent screen damage [Google Support](https://support.google.com/chrome/a/answer/9139543?hl=en-EN).

## Exiting Battery Disconnect Mode

- Plug the Chromebook back into a charger and press the **Power** button to wake it up [Lenovo Support](https://support.lenovo.com/us/en/solutions/ht504333-how-to-prepare-a-chromebook-for-long-term-storage-by-placing-the-machine-in-ship-mode-chromebook).
